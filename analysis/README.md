# Global Technical Book Publication Catalog Analysis

**Scope:** Blockchain · Artificial Intelligence · Quantum Technology · Quantum Computing · Web3 · Cybersecurity (AI/Blockchain) · AI Infrastructure · Quantum Cryptography · Edge AI · Decentralized Systems · Emerging Deep Tech

**Geographic coverage:** United States (federal + state clusters) · Europe (20+ countries) · key global markets (Canada, Israel, UAE, India, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, China, Australia)

**Style:** Consulting-grade strategic report (McKinsey/Bain/BCG analytical posture, Gartner-style forecasting, academic-publishing rigor).

**Methodological note:** This report is a *strategist's synthesis* built from publicly observable patterns in publisher catalogs, university curricula, research funding flows, conference proceedings, and developer-community signals through the cutoff of mid-2026. Numerical scores (1-10) are *relative ordinal estimates*, not surveyed market data. Treat them as a comparative lens, not as audited statistics.

## Conventions used throughout

**Maturity stages (Gartner-style, adapted):**
- **EM** — Emergent (pre-curriculum, mostly papers/blog posts)
- **EA** — Early adopter (first commercial books appearing, fragmented)
- **GR** — Growth (publisher land-grab, beginner glut, advanced gap)
- **MT** — Mature (textbook-stable, evergreen revisions)
- **SAT** — Saturated (oversupply, margin compression)
- **DEC** — Declining (rewrites no longer economic)

**Score axes (1-10):**
- **MO** — Market Opportunity (addressable buyers × willingness to pay)
- **RP** — Revenue Potential (volume × price × shelf-life)
- **AI** — Academic Impact (course adoption + citation potential)
- **RR** — Research Relevance (frontier proximity)
- **LV** — Longevity (years to obsolescence)
- **SC** — Commercial Scalability (translation, course, certification spinoffs)

**Composite Opportunity Score (COS):** weighted mean — `0.25·MO + 0.20·RP + 0.15·AI + 0.10·RR + 0.20·LV + 0.10·SC`.
