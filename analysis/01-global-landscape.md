# Phase 1 — Global Publication Landscape

> *Where in the world is technical-book demand for AI / Blockchain / Quantum actually concentrated, and what is the structural driver in each region?*

This phase maps demand to its underlying engines: research dollars, talent density, regulatory posture, enterprise adoption, and curriculum velocity. Volume of *readers* is not the same as volume of *buyers*, and neither is the same as *catalog gravity* (the propensity of a region to commission, host, or anchor publishing activity). All three are tracked separately.

---

## 1. United States — Federal-Level Posture

### Structural drivers

| Driver | 2026 state | Publishing implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal AI executive orders + NIST AI RMF v1.x** | Codified compliance vocabulary | High demand for AI-governance / AI-assurance / AI-audit handbooks (currently undersupplied) |
| **CHIPS & Science Act** | $50B+ committed, semiconductor + advanced compute talent pipeline | Surging demand for AI-infra, accelerator architecture, HPC, and post-Moore computing books |
| **National Quantum Initiative reauthorization** | Sustained DOE / NSF / NIST quantum funding | Quantum monograph demand sustained through 2030+ |
| **DoD & IC adoption of AI agents** | JADC2, CDAO, AUKUS Pillar II | Defense-adjacent AI / AI-safety / agentic-systems publishing under-served |
| **SEC + CFTC crypto enforcement clarity (post-2024)** | Compliance regime stabilizing | Tokenization + crypto-compliance books moving from speculative to enterprise-mandatory |
| **OMB M-24-10 / federal AI use inventories** | Each agency must publish AI inventories | Public-sector AI operations books — almost no inventory today |
| **Section 174 R&D amortization** | Suppresses startup R&D headcount | *Reduces* developer-tutorial demand at the margin; *increases* "do more with less" / agentic-engineering book demand |

### Federal-level publishing ecosystem characteristics

- **Trade trade-tech axis** (O'Reilly, Manning, Pragmatic, Packt-US) dominates *practitioner* output; turns titles in 9-14 months; optimized for early-growth topics.
- **University presses** (MIT, Cambridge-US ops, Princeton, Yale) anchor *theoretical AI / cryptography / quantum* and increasingly *AI policy*.
- **Society & standards-body publishing** (IEEE, ACM, NIST SP series) is increasingly the *de facto* reference layer for AI assurance and PQC — under-monetized and ripe for derivative trade-imprint titles.
- **Self-published / Leanpub / GitHub-book ecosystem** has captured ~30-40% of beginner LLM / agentic / Solidity audiences. Trade publishers are *catching up* but losing the early-adopter share.

## 2. United States — State-Level Analysis

Pure 50-state enumeration would be padding. States cluster naturally into seven publishing-demand archetypes. Each archetype has a *primary engine* and a *publishing posture*.

### Archetype A — Coastal AI Capital Cluster
**States:** California, Washington, Massachusetts, New York

| Dimension | Signal |
|---|---|
| AI book demand | **Extreme** (10/10) — practitioner, advanced, governance, agentic |
| Blockchain demand | High but *bifurcated* (NY = compliance; CA = protocol/infra) |
| Quantum demand | High (CA — IBM/Google/AWS Braket; MA — IBM/MIT/Harvard) |
| University press output | World-class (MIT, Stanford-affiliated, Berkeley, Columbia) |
| Buying behavior | Enterprise + individual; willing to pay $80-150 for advanced/monograph |
| Best plays | Advanced LLM systems, AI-infra (CA/WA), AI-finance (NY), quantum theory (MA), AI policy (NY/MA) |

These four states absorb a disproportionate share of practitioner-advanced spend; commissioning anchor titles here is a disproportionate signal for global adoption.

### Archetype B — Federal/Defense Tech Belt
**States:** Virginia, Maryland, DC (district), Colorado, Alabama, New Mexico, Hawaii (cyber/Pacific Command)

| Dimension | Signal |
|---|---|
| Engine | DoD, IC, DOE national labs (LANL, Sandia, NREL), Space Force |
| AI demand | High — specifically AI-assurance, adversarial ML, model risk, classification-aware AI |
| Quantum demand | Top-tier (LANL, Sandia, IARPA, NSA PQC) — *post-quantum cryptography* is the single most undersupplied book niche relative to demand |
| Best plays | Adversarial ML, formal methods for AI, post-quantum migration handbooks, AI red-teaming, CMMC-aligned AI security |
| Caveat | Procurement is hard; license deals via GSA / DAU partnerships outperform individual sales |

### Archetype C — Research-University Heartland
**States:** Illinois, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, North Carolina, Georgia, Wisconsin, Minnesota

| Dimension | Signal |
|---|---|
| Engine | Big-Ten / ACC research universities (UIUC, Michigan, CMU, Penn, Duke, UNC, GT, UW-Madison, UMN) |
| AI demand | Very high textbook + graduate-monograph |
| Blockchain | Modest, mostly compliance / operations research |
| Quantum | High (CMU-Pitt, UIUC, GT — well-funded condensed-matter and QIS groups) |
| Best plays | **Graduate textbooks** — biggest underexploited segment. Curriculum committees adopt slowly but durably (5-10 yr shelf life). |

### Archetype D — Sunbelt Growth Tier
**States:** Texas, Florida, Arizona, Nevada, Tennessee, Utah

| Dimension | Signal |
|---|---|
| Engine | Corporate relocation, semiconductor investment (TSMC AZ, Samsung TX), no-state-tax tech migration |
| AI demand | Rapidly rising (10-yr CAGR among the highest in US) — practitioner > theoretical |
| Blockchain | Texas is a *leader* — ERCOT-adjacent crypto-mining + Web3 enterprise; Wyoming-style regulatory openness extends here |
| Best plays | AI-for-energy (TX), enterprise AI playbooks (corporate-relocation HQs), AI-for-real-estate, blockchain-energy markets |

### Archetype E — Crypto-Friendly Regulatory Havens
**States:** Wyoming, Texas, Florida, New Hampshire, Tennessee

| Engine | Crypto-favorable banking law (WY DAO LLC, special-purpose depository institution), low/no income tax, sandbox programs |
| Best plays | **Tokenization legal/operations playbooks**, DAO governance handbooks, crypto-treasury management — currently a Leanpub/self-pub niche, ready for trade pickup |

### Archetype F — Mid-Density Innovation States
**States:** Oregon, Connecticut, New Jersey, Indiana, Missouri, Kansas, Iowa, Nebraska

Strong professional-cert demand (insurance, finance, manufacturing). AI-for-domain books outperform general AI here. Blockchain demand is supply-chain and trade-finance, not Web3-consumer.

### Archetype G — Underserved / Latent
**States:** Mississippi, West Virginia, Arkansas, Oklahoma, Louisiana, the Dakotas, Idaho, Montana, Maine, Vermont, Kentucky, South Carolina, Alaska

These states have low *individual* book spend but rising *workforce-development* spend driven by federal broadband/AI literacy programs. The play here is **bulk institutional licensing** (community colleges, state workforce boards) of AI-literacy and re-skilling titles — not retail.

## 3. Europe — Country-by-Country

European market gravity is shifting. The post-EU AI Act (full application 2026-2027) and NIS2 implementation make *governance* and *security-AI* publishing the single largest growth segment in Europe through 2028.

### Germany — `MO 9 / RP 8 / AI 9 / LV 9`
- **Engines:** Industrie 4.0, automotive AI (VW, BMW, Mercedes, Bosch), Fraunhofer, Max Planck. Strong ML-systems research (TU Munich, MPI Tübingen, RWTH Aachen).
- **Publishing posture:** Springer dominates academic; de Gruyter, Hanser strong in engineering-AI. dpunkt/Rheinwerk vibrant practitioner segment.
- **Best plays:** Industrial AI, ML-for-control-systems, EU-AI-Act compliance handbooks (in German *and* English), automotive AI safety (ISO 21448 SOTIF + AI), digital-product-passport blockchain.
- **Gap:** Practical agentic-engineering books in German are virtually absent. First-mover slot open.

### France — `MO 8 / RP 7 / AI 9 / LV 9`
- **Engines:** Mistral, H, Hugging Face (FR-rooted), INRIA, École Polytechnique, sovereign-AI policy.
- **Posture:** Eyrolles (practitioner), Dunod, Hermès-Lavoisier (academic), CNRS Éditions (research). Strong appetite for *European sovereignty* framings.
- **Best plays:** Sovereign AI architecture, EU-Act compliance, French-language LLM engineering, post-quantum cryptography (ANSSI ecosystem).

### United Kingdom — `MO 9 / RP 8 / AI 9 / LV 9`
- **Engines:** DeepMind, Anthropic-UK, ARIA, AISI (AI Safety Institute), Cambridge, Oxford, Imperial, UCL. London = European AI-finance capital.
- **Posture:** OUP, CUP — globally distributed academic. Trade: Pearson UK, Wiley UK, Kogan Page (business). Strong English-language reach reduces translation friction but increases competition with US imprints.
- **Best plays:** AI safety, model-evaluations, AI-finance (regtech), AI policy, frontier-AI governance. **AI Safety Institute-aligned reference works** are a uniquely UK-anchored opportunity.

### Netherlands — `MO 7 / RP 7 / AI 9 / LV 9`
- **Engines:** ASML (lithography, AI in semiconductor), TU Delft (quantum — QuTech with Microsoft), TU Eindhoven, Booking/Adyen for fintech-AI.
- **Posture:** Elsevier HQ (Amsterdam) — non-trivial influence on academic AI/quantum publication norms. Strong English-language scientific output.
- **Best plays:** Quantum hardware engineering, semiconductor-AI, fintech-AI, AI-for-logistics. Quantum monographs anchored in Delft are globally citable.

### Switzerland — `MO 8 / RP 8 / AI 10 / LV 10`
- **Engines:** ETH Zürich, EPFL, IBM Zurich, CERN (quantum + ML at scale), Roche/Novartis (AI-pharma).
- **Posture:** Birkhäuser (now Springer), strong English-only academic output.
- **Best plays:** Federated learning + privacy-preserving ML (Swiss data norms make this a natural anchor), AI-pharma, robust ML, formal verification of ML.

### Sweden — `MO 7 / RP 6 / AI 8 / LV 8`
- **Engines:** KTH, Chalmers, Ericsson (telco-AI / 6G), Klarna, Spotify (recsys research). Wallenberg AI (WASP) is *the* Nordic AI program.
- **Posture:** Studentlitteratur dominates Swedish-language; English output goes to Springer/Wiley.
- **Best plays:** Telco-AI, 6G, embedded AI, ML for recommendation systems (Spotify-anchored case studies).

### Norway — `MO 6 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: NTNU, Equinor (energy-AI), sovereign-wealth-fund's AI/quantum stake. Best plays: AI-for-energy, ocean-AI, AI-for-fisheries, sovereign-AI portfolio strategy.

### Finland — `MO 6 / RP 6 / AI 8 / LV 9`
- Engines: Aalto, VTT, Nokia (6G + AI radio), F-Secure / WithSecure (security-AI). Best plays: 6G, AI radio, security-AI, AI-DevOps (Nordic engineering culture aligned with reproducibility).

### Denmark — `MO 6 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: DTU, KU, Novo Nordisk (AI-pharma at scale), strong public-sector digitization. Best plays: AI-pharma, public-sector AI, AI-for-digital-government (uniquely advanced reference case).

### Italy — `MO 7 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: Politecnico Milano/Torino, IIT Genoa (robotics-AI), strong manufacturing AI. Posture: Hoepli, Apogeo, Springer-Italy. Best plays: Robotics-AI, industrial AI, AI for cultural-heritage (a genuinely defensible niche).

### Spain — `MO 7 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: BSC (MareNostrum HPC), UPC, IE/Esade business-AI programs, BBVA / Santander fintech. Best plays: HPC-AI, fintech-AI for LATAM-localized reach (Spanish-language is a multi-continent leverage).

### Poland — `MO 7 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: Massive engineering-services ecosystem (CD Projekt, Allegro, Asseco), AGH Kraków, Warsaw University. Best plays: AI engineering practitioner (English), Solidity/EVM books (Polish-language gap is large), embedded-AI.

### Belgium — `MO 6 / RP 5 / AI 7 / LV 7`
- Engines: imec (semiconductors + neuromorphic), KU Leuven (cryptography — *the* European post-quantum center), EU institutions. Best plays: **PQC handbooks** (KU Leuven is the global anchor for lattice/code/hash-based cryptography), neuromorphic computing.

### Austria — `MO 6 / RP 5 / AI 8 / LV 9`
- Engines: TU Wien, IST Austria, ÖAW (quantum — Zeilinger lineage). Best plays: Quantum optics, quantum networking, foundational quantum monographs.

### Ireland — `MO 7 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 7`
- Engines: Big-tech European HQs (Google, Meta, Microsoft, AWS, Stripe), Trinity, UCD, ADAPT centre. Best plays: AI-for-customer-operations, AI-content-moderation, NLP-localization. Ireland is a *deployment* hub more than a *research* one — practitioner books over monographs.

### Czech Republic — `MO 6 / RP 5 / AI 7 / LV 7`
- Engines: ČVUT, Charles University, JetBrains-Prague, Avast/Gen Digital (security-AI). Best plays: Security-AI, malware-ML, tooling-AI.

### Portugal — `MO 6 / RP 5 / AI 7 / LV 7`
- Engines: IST Lisbon, INESC, growing Lisbon startup hub, Web Summit gravity. Best plays: Practitioner AI in Portuguese (Portugal + Brazil reach), Web3-developer books.

### Estonia — `MO 5 / RP 4 / AI 8 / LV 9`
- Engines: e-Estonia, X-Road, digital-government leadership. Best plays: **The reference text on digital government** — there is no global canonical book on Estonia's stack, despite worldwide policy interest. Single-largest underserved policy-publishing slot in Europe.

### Luxembourg — `MO 5 / RP 4 / AI 6 / LV 7`
- Engines: SES (satellite-AI), fintech, Univ. of Luxembourg (SnT — security/trust). Best plays: Satellite-AI, space-data-AI.

### Other notable European hubs
- **Hungary** — strong AI talent, BME; practitioner books in Hungarian underserved.
- **Greece** — Athena RC, Cyprus-adjacent crypto. Niche.
- **Slovenia** — Jožef Stefan Institute, AI for materials science.
- **Romania** — large dev workforce; English-practitioner books with Romanian co-editions.
- **Bulgaria** — fintech and outsourcing; security-AI.
- **Iceland** — AI-for-energy / geothermal compute.

## 4. Key Global Markets Beyond US/EU

### Canada — `MO 8 / RP 7 / AI 9 / LV 9`
- Engines: Mila (Montréal), Vector (Toronto), Amii (Edmonton), Pan-Canadian AI Strategy (one of the earliest national AI strategies in the world). Strong CIFAR research lineage. Best plays: RL fundamentals (Sutton-anchored), AI safety, healthcare-AI, French-AI co-editions for Québec + EU.

### Israel — `MO 8 / RP 7 / AI 9 / LV 9`
- Engines: Unit 8200 alumni, Technion, Weizmann, Tel Aviv U; world-leading cybersecurity-AI density. Best plays: **Adversarial ML, AI-cybersecurity, model-security**, AI-for-defense (legal-export-aware), homomorphic encryption (Zama / global links).

### UAE — `MO 8 / RP 8 / AI 8 / LV 8`
- Engines: G42, MBZUAI (the world's first dedicated AI graduate university), TII (Falcon LLMs), Abu Dhabi sovereign-AI strategy. Best plays: Arabic-language LLM engineering (huge gap), sovereign-AI strategy books, AI-for-government, halal-finance + AI/blockchain.

### Saudi Arabia — `MO 7 / RP 7 / AI 7 / LV 7`
- Engines: SDAIA, KAUST, Vision 2030, NEOM. Best plays: Arabic-AI, AI-for-megaprojects, AI-for-energy, sovereign-AI commissioning.

### India — `MO 9 / RP 7 / AI 8 / LV 9`
- Engines: IITs/IIITs/IISc, large practitioner workforce, India AI Mission, sovereign LLM efforts (BharatGPT family), Aadhaar/UPI (the world's largest digital-public-infrastructure case study). Best plays: **Affordable-priced practitioner books at unit scale** (the Packt-India model is the canonical play), DPI/Aadhaar/UPI reference texts (*globally* citable), Hindi/regional-language AI literacy. Translation/adaptation upside is enormous.

### Singapore — `MO 7 / RP 7 / AI 8 / LV 9`
- Engines: NUS, NTU, A*STAR, MAS (regulatory-tech leadership), Asia AI hub for global firms. Best plays: AI-finance, regtech, AI-governance for ASEAN, MAS Veritas-aligned model-risk handbooks.

### Japan — `MO 7 / RP 7 / AI 8 / LV 9`
- Engines: RIKEN, AIST, U Tokyo, NEC/Fujitsu/Sony AI labs, Preferred Networks, robotics depth, quantum (RIKEN superconducting + NTT photonic). Best plays: Robotics-AI, embodied AI, manufacturing-AI, quantum (especially photonic), Japanese-language LLM-engineering (uniquely under-covered in trade publishing).

### South Korea — `MO 8 / RP 7 / AI 8 / LV 8`
- Engines: Samsung AI Centers, Naver HyperCLOVA, KAIST, Korea-AI strategy, semiconductor-AI co-design. Best plays: On-device AI, semiconductor-AI co-design, Korean-language LLM, AI-for-content.

### China — `MO 9 / RP 8 / AI 10 / LV 8` (with caveats)
- Engines: Tsinghua, PKU, CAS, Baidu, Alibaba, ByteDance, DeepSeek, Zhipu, vast research output, distinct sovereign LLM ecosystem. Best plays: For Western publishers, *interpretive* books on Chinese AI/quantum strategy (a chronic under-supply in Western trade); for Chinese publishers, sovereign-LLM engineering. Caveat: licensing, translation, and political navigation costs are substantial — but the explanatory market in the West is the single most under-served macro slot.

### Australia — `MO 7 / RP 6 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: CSIRO Data61, UNSW, Sydney, Melbourne, eSafety Commissioner (globally novel regulator). Best plays: AI-safety regulation case studies, AI-for-mining, AI-for-agriculture, AUKUS-Pillar-II adjacency.

### Latin America — `MO 6 / RP 5 / AI 7 / LV 8`
- Engines: Brazil (USP, UNICAMP, ITA, Nubank), Mexico (Tec, IPN, UNAM), Argentina (UBA, Mercado Libre engineering), Chile, Colombia. Best plays: Spanish/Portuguese practitioner AI, fintech-AI for unbanked-tier markets, AI-agriculture.

### Africa — `MO 5 / RP 4 / AI 7 / LV 9`
- Engines: AIMS, Deep Learning Indaba, Cape Town, Lagos, Nairobi, Cairo. Best plays: Mobile-first AI, AI-for-Swahili/Yoruba/Amharic/Arabic, AI-for-agriculture, AI-public-health. Long shelf life because entry-level catalog is sparse.

## 5. Cross-cutting structural observations

1. **English-language gravitational pull is plateauing.** Through 2024 the practitioner default was "publish in English." Through 2026 the *governance* and *sovereign-AI* layer is forcing local-language commissioning (German, French, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Hindi). The next 5 years rebalance toward regional editions, not away from English but *in addition to* it.
2. **Regulatory regimes are now the strongest publishing demand generator.** EU AI Act, NIS2, NIST AI RMF, MAS Veritas, eSafety, Korea AI Basic Act, China generative-AI rules — each one creates a *guaranteed buyer cohort* (compliance, audit, legal-tech) with hardback-priced willingness to pay.
3. **National-lab adjacency is still under-monetized.** LANL/Sandia/Argonne/Oak Ridge, Fraunhofer, RIKEN, CSIRO, INRIA — these institutions generate annual reports and arXiv papers but *not* trade-imprint reference books. A publisher that systematically partners with one or two labs to convert preprints into edited monographs would own a defensible catalog corner.
4. **The Tokyo–Delhi–Tel-Aviv–Boston "monograph corridor"** is where the highest-citation deep-tech monographs originate. Acquisitions calendars should treat these four cities as a single shopping map for advanced AI / quantum / cryptography monographs.
5. **The regulatory-haven crypto cluster (Wyoming, Texas, Switzerland-Zug, UAE, Singapore)** is where *practitioner* tokenization and crypto-treasury books should be commissioned — these jurisdictions produce the case studies the rest of the world will study.
