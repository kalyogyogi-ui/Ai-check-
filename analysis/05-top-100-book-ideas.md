# Phase 5 — Top 100 Future Book Ideas

> *Concrete, commissionable book concepts with title, subtitle, audience, USP, suggested publisher, and Composite Opportunity Score.*

These 100 ideas are calibrated to **2026-2030** publication windows. The pipeline distributes as: **A. Commercial / Practitioner** (40), **B. Academic textbooks** (25), **C. Research monographs / advanced** (20), **D. Business / executive / policy** (15).

Each entry uses the COS rubric from [Phase 4](./04-market-gaps.md). USP is intentionally one sentence — if a book's distinguishing thesis cannot be expressed in one sentence, it cannot be marketed in one either.

**Format key:** SPB = senior practitioner playbook · CB = cookbook · IA = in-action · GT = graduate textbook · UT = undergraduate textbook · RM = research monograph · EB = executive briefing · CP = certification prep.
