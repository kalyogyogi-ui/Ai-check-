# Social Media Growth Engine 🚀

## Objective
Generate viral-potential threads, hooks, and posts for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Instagram for a specific niche.

## The Prompt
> **"You are a Viral Social Media Architect and Growth Hacker. I need a high-impact content pack for [PLATFORM] about [TOPIC].
> 
> Please generate:
> 1. **5 High-Intensity Hooks**: These must stop the scroll. Use techniques like 'The Contradiction', 'The Revelation', or 'The Expert Secret'.
> 2. **1 Long-Form Thread/Post**: 
> - If X: A 10-part thread with a satisfying payoff.
> - If LinkedIn: A 'Value-Add' post with clear line breaks and a strong personal take.
> 3. **Engagement Strategy**: Suggest a question to ask at the end to maximize comments.
> 4. **Visual Direction**: Describe the type of image or video that should accompany this post.
>
> Constraints: 
> - No clichés (e.g., 'In today's fast-paced world...').
> - Use 'punchy' sentences.
> - Focus on high utility or high emotion."**

## Recommended Model
- Gemini 3.1 Pro (Optimized for creative engagement)
- GPT-5.3 Instant (Best for viral phrasing)
- Claude Sonnet 4.6

## Tips
For LinkedIn, prioritize the first 3 lines. That is where users decide whether to click 'See More'.
