# SaaS Unit Economics Analyzer 📊

## Objective
Analyze a company's financial metrics to determine the health of its business model and suggest optimizations.

## The Prompt
> **"You are a Senior Venture Capital Partner and SaaS Operations Expert. Analyze the following unit economics for [COMPANY_NAME]:
> [INSERT_METRICS: e.g., CAC, LTV, Churn, ARPU, MRR Growth].
> 
> Please calculate and interpret:
> 1. **LTV/CAC Ratio**: Is the customer acquisition strategy sustainable?
> 2. **CAC Payback Period**: How many months until the company breaks even on a new user?
> 3. **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Is the company growing through expansion or just new sales?
> 4. **Rule of 40**: Does the combination of growth rate and profit margin exceed 40%?
>
> Provide:
> - **Red Flags**: Any metrics that indicate deep trouble.
> - **Optimization Levers**: 3 specific actions to improve these numbers.
> - **Benchmark Comparison**: How do these numbers compare to 'Best-in-Class' SaaS companies?"**

## Recommended Model
- Gemini 3.1 Pro (Excellent for quantitative analysis)
- GPT-5.4 Pro
- Claude Opus 4.6 (Nuanced financial context)

## Tips
If specific metrics aren't available, ask the model to estimate based on industry averages for that specific niche.
