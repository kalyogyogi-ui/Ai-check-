# Market Disruption Audit 📉

## Objective
Analyze a specific industry or company to identify vulnerabilities and opportunities for AI-driven disruption.

## The Prompt
> **"You are a Disruptive Technology Analyst and Venture Investment Strategist. Your task is to perform a comprehensive 'Market Disruption Audit' for the [INDUSTRY/COMPANY]. 
> 
> Please analyze the following:
> 1. **Incumbent Vulnerabilities**: Where are the high margins, high friction, and low customer satisfaction areas?
> 2. **AI Wedge**: How could a new entrant use LLMs or Agentic AI to solve a core problem 10x cheaper or faster?
> 3. **Regulatory Moats**: What legal or compliance barriers exist, and how can they be navigated or bypassed?
> 4. **Distribution Advantages**: How would a disruptor reach customers without outspending the incumbent on marketing?
> 5. **Scenario Planning**: Define two scenarios:
> - Scenario A: The incumbent adapts successfully.
> - Scenario B: The incumbent is 'Blockbustered'.
>
> Provide a 'Disruption Score' (1-10) and a list of 'First Principles' to guide a startup in this space."**

## Recommended Model
- Gemini 3.1 Pro (Deep market logic)
- GPT-5.4 Pro
- Claude Opus 4.6 (Best for nuanced scenario planning)

## Example Usage
Used by 38shift to evaluate new venture ideas or consulting clients' competitive landscapes.
